My Language Portrait

Every language is part of who we are. Show where your languages live in you!

How to complete your portrait
  1. Write your name. In the palette, type a language next to each colour you want to use (for example, Arabic, English, Hindi, or a language you are learning).
  2. Click a colour, then click parts of the person to colour them. You can colour the head, eyes, mouth, heart, body, arms, hands and legs. Choose colours that feel meaningful to you.
  3. Write a few words about your choices. Why did you put each language in those places?
  4. Click Save my completed portrait. Attach the downloaded .html file to an email and send it back to me.
Where to find your saved portrait

After clicking Save my completed portrait, look in your device’s Downloads folder. Your file will be named something like Language_Portrait_YourName.html.

  • Windows: Open File Explorer → Downloads.
  • Mac: Open Finder → Downloads.
  • iPhone or iPad: Open the Files app → Browse → Downloads.
  • Android: Open Files or My Files → Downloads.

You can also check the download arrow in your browser. To return your work, reply to the activity email, attach your saved .html file from Downloads, and send it.

Tip: Save your completed copy before closing this page. You can reopen your saved file and continue editing it.
